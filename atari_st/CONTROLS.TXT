THE LEGEND OF ZELDA FOR THE ATARI ST
====================================

Run ZELDAST.PRG. It needs 512 KB of RAM
and the .DAT files beside it.

CONTROLS
--------
Walk        Arrow keys, W A S D,
            or a joystick in port 1
Sword       Fire, Control or Shift
Use item    Space or Alternate
Inventory   Tab (Left/Right choose
            the item for Space)
Start,      Return, Enter or Fire
choose
Pause,      Esc (then Return to
save, quit  pick a line)
Help        This list, in the game

In the file menu: Return starts,
Tab moves to the next line, Control
or Fire types the chosen letter.

The status bar shows the keys too:
SPACE over the item, and over the
sword the fire key you used last
(FIRE, CTRL or SHFT).

The game is saved to ZELDA.SAV on
the same disk, from the Esc menu or
when Link dies.
