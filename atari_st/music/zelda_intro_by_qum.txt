Packed YM2149 stream, 4174 frames @ 50 Hz, 11713 bytes, 1 digidrum(s), table 256 bytes.
See the comment at the top of ymplay.h.

Digidrums: call ymdrum_init(zelda_intro_by_qum_drums) once and ymdrum_exit() at the end, both in supervisor mode.
They use MFP Timer D (vector $110, level 6); nothing else may use Timer D while the music plays.
Call ymplay_step(); ymplay_write(); every 50 Hz tick from supervisor mode. ymplay_write() leaves the volume
register of the voice that plays a drum alone; if you write the PSG yourself, do the same (see ymdrum_busy()).

No timer effects in this song.
